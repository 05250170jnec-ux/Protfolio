About Me

I am Dechen Pem, a Diploma in Computer System and Network student at Jigme Namgyel Engineering College, Royal University of Bhutan. I am curious about how data travels between devices, how networks are planned and addressed, and what it takes to keep systems running smoothly and securely.

Alongside networking, I am learning web development with HTML, CSS and JavaScript. It lets me turn ideas into working pages and present my work to others.
